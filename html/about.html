THE STORY / 001

NOT JUST
A NAME.
A FEELING.

Founded in 1964 as a subsidiary of Warner Bros. Records, Loma Records became associated with soul and R&B releases. This conceptual revival reimagines that musical heritage as a contemporary visual system.

Bold type, sharp geometry, and rhythmic motion take cues from the label’s original graphic language.
